The part nothing else does 02 / 06

Send part of a form, not all of it.

Sometimes someone else needs to see your answers — an accountant, a caseworker, a relative helping with the paperwork. FormForLater can send them a link instead of forwarding everything. It's encrypted before it leaves your browser, and you pick what's in it, field by field.

  • Encrypted on your device. AES-256-GCM. The key never leaves the link.
  • Expires on its own. An hour, a day, a week — or set it to delete itself after the first open.
  • No account needed. Just a link and a browser.
  • Left out, not just hidden. Fields you hold back are removed before encryption. They're never in the link at all.

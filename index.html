The part nothing else does 02 / 06

Hand off part of a form, not all of it.

Send a link instead of forwarding everything. It’s encrypted before it leaves your browser, and you choose what travels, field by field.

  • Encrypted here. AES-256-GCM. The key never leaves the link.
  • Expires on schedule. An hour, a day, a week — and can delete itself on first open.
  • No account needed. A link and a browser. Nothing to install.
  • Held back, not hidden. Removed before encryption — never present to leak.
